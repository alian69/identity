# Elon Musk

## Overview

**Elon Reeve Musk** was born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa. He later became a Canadian citizen and an American citizen. He is an entrepreneur and business executive whose work has been associated with electric vehicles, commercial spaceflight, satellite internet, artificial intelligence, and other technology fields.

## Education

Musk attended Queen's University in Canada before transferring to the University of Pennsylvania in the United States. Tesla's 2026 proxy statement describes him as holding a B.A. in Physics and a B.S. in Business from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

## Career and companies

- **Zip2:** Musk co-founded the online city-guide software company Zip2. Compaq acquired it in 1999.
- **X.com and PayPal:** He co-founded the online financial-services company X.com. X.com later became part of PayPal, which eBay acquired in 2002.
- **SpaceX:** Musk founded Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) in 2002. Tesla's 2026 proxy statement lists him as SpaceX's Chief Executive Officer, Chief Technology Officer, and Chairman.
- **Tesla:** He joined Tesla's leadership early in the company's history and has served as its Chief Executive Officer since October 2008, according to Tesla's 2026 proxy statement.
- **SolarCity:** He served as chairman of SolarCity from 2006 until Tesla acquired the company in 2016.
- **X and xAI:** Musk has held leadership roles connected with X, the social-media company formerly known as Twitter, and the artificial-intelligence company xAI. Tesla's 2026 proxy statement says X and xAI were combined under X.AI Holdings in 2025 and that X.AI Holdings became a subsidiary of SpaceX on February 2, 2026.
- **Other ventures:** He is also a founder of The Boring Company, which works on infrastructure and tunnelling, and Neuralink, which develops brain-computer interface technology.

## Areas of work

- Electric vehicles and energy products
- Rockets, spacecraft, and launch services
- Satellite-based internet services
- Artificial intelligence
- Brain-computer interface research
- Tunnelling and infrastructure projects
